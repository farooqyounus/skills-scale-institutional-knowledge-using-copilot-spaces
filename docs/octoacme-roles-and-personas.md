# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support team health, delivery execution, and technical alignment. They help translate strategy into actionable plans while protecting team capacity, quality, and velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Align engineering capacity with roadmap and release priorities
- Support sprint planning, dependency management, and team-level execution
- Coach engineering staff and help resolve delivery bottlenecks
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on scope and sequencing
- Monitor technical quality, operational risks, and cross-team dependencies

### Goals
- Maintain predictable engineering delivery
- Improve team health, focus, and execution quality
- Reduce friction between product intent and engineering implementation

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering and roadmap syncs
- Sprint health reviews and capacity discussions
- Escalation points for resourcing, quality, or delivery risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to support execution and team productivity
- Coordinates with Product Managers on priority trade-offs and sequencing
- Partners with Project Managers to keep milestones, workload, and dependencies realistic

---

## QA / Release Quality Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Release Quality Leads ensure the team ships work that meets acceptance criteria, is safe to release, and is supported by clear validation and readiness checks.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality gates and acceptance validation plans
- Coordinate regression, smoke, and release-readiness testing
- Work with Developers to identify and close defects before release
- Verify that business and technical requirements are testable and complete
- Partner with Project Managers and Product Managers on release confidence and readiness

### Goals
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Improve confidence in release quality and stakeholder trust
- Make quality criteria visible and measurable across the team

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and validation status updates
- Cross-functional signoff for final release approvals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers on test coverage, bug triage, and validation evidence
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and feature readiness
- Works with Project Managers to coordinate release timing, QA checkpoints, and risk communication

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Leads safeguard the project against technical, privacy, and policy risks. They help teams identify and manage security concerns early in the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review workflows, architectures, and data handling for security and compliance risks
- Define required controls, review checkpoints, and escalation paths
- Support policy compliance and secure-by-default implementation practices
- Coordinate with engineering and leadership on risk mitigation and incident response
- Provide guidance on security considerations for roadmap decisions and release readiness

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure
- Ensure projects meet required controls and stakeholder expectations
- Improve readiness for audits, incident response, and safe deployment

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews and security checkpoints
- Compliance and incident escalation updates
- Security guidance in planning, design, and release reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers and Engineering Managers on secure implementation and mitigation planning
- Supports Product Managers and Project Managers with risk framing and communication for stakeholders
- Escalates critical concerns to leadership and cross-functional partners when business impact is significant

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison roles connect delivery work to customer needs, stakeholder expectations, and communication outcomes. They help translate business context into actionable project updates and user value signals.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer feedback and stakeholder concerns that affect project priorities
- Communicate release impact, status, and changes to relevant stakeholders
- Support adoption planning, user communication, and issue escalation where needed
- Help validate whether delivered work meets stakeholder expectations and business value
- Coordinate follow-up between product teams and customer-facing groups

### Goals
- Improve customer trust and stakeholder alignment
- Ensure delivery decisions reflect user and business realities
- Reduce communication gaps between engineering and customer-facing teams

### Typical Communication
- Weekly stakeholder updates and customer-facing summaries
- Release communication and impact assessments
- Feedback loops from customers, support, or sales channels

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Shares customer insights with Product Managers and Developers to improve prioritization and solutions
- Works with Project Managers to align updates, milestones, and escalations
- Helps Product Managers communicate value and adoption signals from the field

---

## Data / Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Owners enable evidence-based decisions by measuring product performance, validating outcomes, and tracking adoption or customer impact.

### Responsibilities
- Define key success metrics and data collection needs
- Monitor product usage, quality signals, and operational dashboards
- Validate whether launched work meets intended business and customer outcomes
- Support analysis for experiments, release decisions, and continuous improvement
- Provide reporting and trend analysis to the team and stakeholders

### Goals
- Improve decision quality through measurable evidence
- Track outcomes beyond feature delivery to actual value realization
- Support objective review of progress, quality, and impact

### Typical Communication
- Reporting and dashboard reviews
- Metric reviews during planning and release retrospectives
- Outcome tracking with Product and leadership stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Product Managers define measurable success criteria
- Partners with Developers and QA leads to understand quality signals and user impact
- Provides Project Managers with objective status indicators and risk signals for reporting

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intended to clarify ownership, improve cross-functional handoffs, and make accountability more explicit across delivery, quality, security, and stakeholder communication.
